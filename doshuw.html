Doshuw 585 (SOM) — Gold-Backed Digital Assets

Doshuw 585, trading under the symbol SOM, is a digital token backed by physical gold (583–585 purity jewelry and scrap gold) held in secure storage in Ingushetia (Russian Federation).

A Stable Store of Value

Doshuw SOM provides a reliable and stable way to preserve wealth. Each token is pegged to 1 gram of gold (minimum 583 purity, 14 karat).
